Plan

Adding marketplaces without rewriting Phase 1.

The engine from Phase 1 is shared: duplicate rules, mapping, fill engine, link capture and outbox. Each new marketplace adds one adapter, its mapping rules, a sale check and removal steps.

Suggested order

Step 1

eBay (API)

  • OAuth connect, business policies, category suggestions
  • List, withdraw, poll orders
  • Most reliable sales signal of the six
Step 2

Mercari

  • Browser adapter: 12 photos, 1,000-character description
  • No SKU field: lost-link check matches title + price
Step 3

Depop

  • No title field: title leads the description
  • 8 photos, SKU up to 50
  • Partner API if approved
Step 4

Vinted

  • 20 photos
  • Identical items can’t be listed twice
  • Pro Integrations if allowlisted
Step 5

Facebook Marketplace

  • 10 photos, aria-labelled form
  • Local pickup sales leave no reliable sold signal: you mark it sold in ReSellit
Then

Sales and removal

  • Sale check per marketplace, ledger in ReSellit
  • Remove the rest after your OK
  • Double-sale alert

Removing sold items: the honest trade-off

Only eBay can report a sale to ReSellit; on the other marketplaces the extension checks your sold items while Chrome is open. The gap between checks is when a second buyer can purchase the same item. Scenario 3 shows how ReSellit flags that.

Shorter checks narrow the gap but mean more page loads on your account. A 10–15 minute check while you work is the suggested start.

Removals run one at a time, and only after you approve them. Poshmark dropped its Excessive Listing Removal Policy on July 23, 2026, so deleting an item that sold elsewhere isn’t penalized. “Not For Sale” is still a softer option.

What stays manual, on purpose

  • Pressing List on browser marketplaces: you review each form.
  • Approving removals after a sale.
  • Cancelling the second order in a double sale.
  • Marking a Facebook pickup sale as sold.
